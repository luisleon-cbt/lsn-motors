LSN-MOTORS interactives

The interactives for this lesson: classroom activities that explain the ideas, and lab simulators to practice before you touch the real equipment.

Activity 1 · Classroom · 45 min

Why the rotor slows its own voltage down

Run a motor from standstill to full speed in the rotating field lab and fill in the three-state matrix: slip, voltage, rotor frequency, current, power factor, and torque.

Open Rotating field labClassroom
Activity 2 · Classroom · 45 min

Read the nameplate

Solve ten speed and slip problems, then work out the poles, slip, and rotor frequency for four motors at Biscayne Cold Storage.

Open Slip and speed challengeClassroom
Activity 3 · Lab · 75 min

Measure speed, slip, and rotor frequency

Practice on the motor test bench, then measure the trainer motor at several loads with a tachometer and a clamp meter, and calculate slip and rotor frequency.

Open Motor test benchLab
Activity 4 · Lab · 75 min

Starting current and speed control with a VFD

Capture the inrush current of an across-the-line start, then run the motor from a VFD at 30, 45, and 60 Hz and compare speed, slip, and current.
