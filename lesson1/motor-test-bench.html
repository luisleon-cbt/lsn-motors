How to use this lab

  1. Read the five rules in Safety check below and tick every box. The instructor will not start the trainer until all five are ticked.
  2. In Bench controls, press Apply tape while the trainer is locked out. A small square of reflective tape appears on the shaft on the board above. The laser tachometer counts one pulse each time the tape passes.
  3. Press Instructor: energize to start the motor. Leave the load at 0% and press Take reading. The speed (RPM) and the current (A) show on the meters and in the first row of the results table.
  4. Press the next load button, 25%, then press Take reading. Repeat for 50%, 75%, 100%, and 125%. You are done when the results table has six rows.
  5. In the results table, type the slip s (how far the rotor falls behind the field, as a decimal), the % slip, and fr (the rotor frequency) for every row. Press Check calculations. A red box comes with the worked math under the table: fix it and check again.
  6. Look at the graph next to the table: the speed falls and the current climbs as the load grows. Answer the four Questions below it. Your score out of 100 is in Your score.

Things to notice

  • Ns stays at 1800 RPM at every load. Only the rotor speed changes, so the slip grows with the load.
  • Each 25% of load costs about 20 RPM, so speed against load is close to a straight line up to full load.
  • The current does not start at zero. Close to 1 A at no load is the magnetizing current that builds the field (0.4 × FLA in this model).
  • At 125% the speed only drops to about 1704 RPM, but the current passes the FLA of 2.4 A. The clamp meter shows an overload better than the tachometer does.
  • The shaft on screen turns slowly so you can see it, and the change with load is exaggerated. The tachometer shows the real number.
