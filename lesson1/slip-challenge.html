Words to know and the teaching model

Ns (synchronous speed) is how fast the stator's magnetic field turns. Nr is the rotor speed, read with a tachometer. Slip is how far the rotor falls behind the field, as a part of Ns. Rotor frequency (fr) is how many times a second the rotor currents change direction. FLA is the full-load amps on the nameplate. A VFD (variable frequency drive) changes the frequency sent to the motor.

The current and power factor rounds use the lesson's simplified teaching model (rotor resistance R2 = 0.04 and standstill reactance X20 = 0.20, per unit). It leaves out the stator resistance and other losses. Real motors draw roughly 5 to 7 times full-load current at start.
